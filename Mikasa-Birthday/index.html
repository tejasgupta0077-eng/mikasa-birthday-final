✦ OUR LITTLE STORY ✦

How it

01

THE BEGINNING

It started before you knew...

Hamari story shayad uss time se start ho gayi thi jab tumhe iska pata bhi nahi tha.

Main tumhe almost 2 saal se like karta tha. Class 10 ki starting se hi pata nahi kab tumhe dekhte-dekhte feelings aa gayi.

Lekin kabhi bolne ki himmat nahi hui.

Jab bhi tumhe dekhta tha na, ekdum se ajeeb si butterfly feeling hoti thi. Kuch seconds ke liye literally dimaag blank ho jata tha aur samajh hi nahi aata tha kya bolu. 😂

Itne time tak ye sab apne andar rakha, aur finally class 12 mein tumhe bata hi diya.

Shayad thoda late tha, thoda nervous bhi tha, but I'm genuinely glad ki maine finally bol diya. ❤️

02

THAT SNAPCHAT CALL

The first little memory

Hamari first Snapchat video call. Call muted thi, main nervous tha... aur phir funny filters ne situation sambhal li. 😂

03

EREN & MIKASA

And then came the nicknames...

Attack on Titan recommend kiya tha, lekin pata nahi tha ki ek din main Eren ban jaunga aur tum Mikasa. ⚔️

04

LITTLE THINGS

Random conversations

Phir dheere-dheere hamari random conversations start hui.

Kabhi faltu ki baatein, kabhi bina kisi reason ke hours tak baat, aur kabhi ekdum random topic se pata nahi kaha tak pahunch jana. 😂

Dheere-dheere hum ek dusre ke baare mein chhoti-chhoti cheezein jaanne lage.

Jo baatein normally kisi ko nahi batate, woh bhi ek dusre se share karne lage. Thodi oversharing bhi hui, obviously. 😭😂

Aur funny baat ye hai ki wahi faltu ki baatein mujhe sabse zyada achhi lagne lagi.

Pata hi nahi chala kab random conversations actual memories ban gayi. ❤️

05

SOMEWHERE ALONG THE WAY

You became important.

Aur shayad yahin se sab thoda different hone laga.

Tumse baar-baar baat karte karte mujhe tumse ek deep connection feel hone laga.

Ye sirf ek normal liking nahi thi.

Main genuinely tumhe like karne laga, tumhari care karne laga aur dheere-dheere tumse love bhi karne laga. ❤️

Ab tumse baat karna bas ek habit nahi hai. Tumse baat karna genuinely achha lagta hai, aur tumhari presence mere liye actually matter karti hai.

06

AND THIS IS ONLY THE BEGINNING

There is still more to our story...

Aur honestly... abhi toh hamari story ki starting hi hui hai.

Aage sab kuch hamesha perfect rahe, zaroori nahi.

Kuch struggles aayenge, misunderstandings hongi, kabhi difficult days bhi honge.

But chahe jo bhi ho, main chahta hoon ki hum ek dusre par easily give up na karein.

Problems aaye toh unse bhaagne ke bajaye ek dusre ko samajhne ki koshish karein.

Main hamesha tumhari respect karunga, tumhari feelings ki value karunga aur kabhi jaan-bujhkar tumhe low feel nahi hone dunga.

Main chahta hoon ki tum mere saath hamesha comfortable raho aur bina kisi hesitation ke khud jaisi reh sako. ❤️

Kyunki mujhe nahi lagta ki hamari story sirf kuch memories tak rehni chahiye.

Humein abhi saath mein bahut door tak jaana hai.

Abhi bahut saari memories banani hain, bahut saari random baatein karni hain, bahut hasna hai aur pata nahi kitne chapters abhi baaki hain.

Ye end nahi hai... ye toh bas beginning hai. ❤️
